# Bezier Clock: An Analog Clock That Makes Time Feel Like Motion

**Bezier Clock is a full-screen analog clock app for iPhone and iPad.** Its defining detail is a set of smooth curves connecting the hour, minute, and second hands. Instead of presenting time only as numbers, it turns the changing relationship between the hands into a moving visual composition.

## What makes Bezier Clock different?

Most clock apps show familiar hands on a fixed dial. Bezier Clock draws attention to the space *between* the hands. Curves connect the hour, minute, and second positions, creating a shape that changes as time passes. The result is both a clock and a subtle piece of motion design.

That visual idea is the app's main advantage. It gives a spare desk setup a focal point while preserving the recognizable language of an analog clock. If you prefer watching time move to reading large digital numerals, this is the appeal.

## A clock designed for the space around you

Place an iPhone on a desk stand to keep the time within sight while you work. An iPad gives the curves more room to breathe and can serve as a larger clock for a shelf or bedside table. The display supports both portrait and landscape layouts, so you can position the device to fit your setup.

Swipe to move between light and dark themes. A lighter look can suit a bright workspace; the dark theme offers a quieter appearance in a dimmer room. Screen brightness and sleep behavior still depend on your device settings.
