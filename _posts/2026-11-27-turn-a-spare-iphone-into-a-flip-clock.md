# Turn a Spare iPhone or iPad Into a Flip Clock

**If you have a compatible iPhone or iPad sitting unused, ZenFlip can turn it into a dedicated, full-screen flip clock.** The app shows large digits with a retro flip animation, supports light and dark themes, and lets you hide seconds for a quieter display. It is a straightforward way to give a spare device a useful place on your desk or nightstand.

![ZenFlip app icon](/assets/img/flipclock.png)

## Why use a spare device as a clock?

A phone or tablet on a stand can keep the time visible without interrupting what you are doing on your main screen. At a desk, a glance is enough to check the hour. Beside a bed, a larger iPad display can make the clock easier to see from a distance. ZenFlip's full-screen layout puts the time at the center of that experience.

This is especially appealing if you like the look of a classic flip clock but already own a device that can display one. Check the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6443968056) to confirm compatibility with your particular iPhone or iPad.

## A simple setup for a desk or nightstand

1. Install ZenFlip on a compatible iPhone or iPad.
2. Put the device on a stable stand where the screen is easy to see.
3. Open the app and choose the orientation that fits your space; the clock layout adjusts to the screen.
4. Tap to show or hide seconds, and swipe to choose a light or dark theme.
5. Adjust brightness, charging, and screen sleep behavior in your device settings to suit where you use it.

The app remembers your seconds preference and selected appearance. Its main display hides the status bar, keeping attention on the clock face. The flip animation gives each change of time a familiar, tactile feel without adding extra steps to daily use.
