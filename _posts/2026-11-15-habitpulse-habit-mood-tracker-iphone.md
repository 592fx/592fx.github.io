# HabitPulse: Track Your Habits and Mood in One Place

**HabitPulse is an iPhone habit and mood tracker designed to make daily reflection simple.** It combines a daily habit checklist, quick mood entries, progress charts, and private on-device analysis. If you want to understand how your routines and feelings change together, keeping both records in one app makes that easier.

![HabitPulse app icon](/assets/img/habitpulse.png)

## Why track habits and mood together?

A streak tells you whether you completed a habit. A mood entry adds context to the day. HabitPulse lets you review both over time, including descriptive correlation and lag patterns between completed habits and later mood entries. These patterns are observations, **not evidence that a habit caused a mood change**.

For example, you could track a short evening walk, record how you feel each day, and later inspect whether those entries tend to appear together. That gives you a starting point for reflection without turning your routine into a complicated spreadsheet.

## Features that support a practical routine

- Create daily habits or custom weekly schedules, with optional reminders.
- Mark habits complete and review history, streaks, weekly statistics, and calendar heatmaps.
- Record your mood quickly and review recent trends.
- Use colors and icons to make habits easy to recognize.
- Export data and share achievement or heatmap images from your device.

HabitPulse stores habit and mood data on your device. Its analysis and exports are generated locally, and optional Face ID protection can add privacy for your journal. HabitPulse Pro adds unlimited habits, extended statistics, pattern analysis, alternate app icons, and additional exports through a one-time purchase.

Try HabitPulse if you are looking for a **simple habit tracker with mood tracking**, a **private journal for routines**, or an iPhone app that helps you see progress without a crowded interface.
