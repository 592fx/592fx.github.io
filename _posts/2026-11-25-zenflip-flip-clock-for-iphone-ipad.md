# ZenFlip: A Full-Screen Flip Clock for iPhone and iPad

**ZenFlip is a minimalist flip clock app for iPhone and iPad.** It displays large, easy-to-read digits with a smooth retro flip animation, making your device useful as a desk clock or bedside clock. You can show or hide seconds and switch between light and dark themes with a swipe.

## What does ZenFlip do?

ZenFlip turns your screen into a full-screen time display. Its focus is the clock itself: prominent digits, a classic flipping motion, and a simple interface that is easy to read at a glance. Place an iPhone beside your keyboard while working, or set an iPad on a stand where you can see the time from across the room.

## Key features

- **Full-screen flip clock:** Large digits and a retro flip animation keep the time visible.
- **Optional seconds:** Tap the clock to show or hide the seconds display.
- **Light and dark themes:** Swipe to change the appearance to suit your space.
- **iPhone and iPad support:** Use the device you already have as a desk or bedside clock.
- **Simple access:** No account is required.

## Who is ZenFlip for?

ZenFlip suits anyone who wants a dedicated clock display while studying, working, or relaxing. The large numbers work particularly well on a desk or nightstand. If you prefer a quieter look, hide the seconds; if you want a different appearance, switch themes with a swipe.

For example, prop an iPhone beside a laptop for an easy-to-glance-at work clock. On a bedside table, switch to the dark theme and hide seconds for a simpler display. An iPad on a stand gives you a larger clock face for a room.
